# Text-to-Image Prompts

## Prompt 1 (Login Security)

A hyper-realistic, 8K, cinematic shot of a futuristic digital fortress at night. The fortress is made of dark, hexagonal structures, with glowing neon blue data streams flowing across its surface. In the background, a vast, dark server room with glowing racks of servers. The overall mood is dark, mysterious, and secure.

## Prompt 2 (Login Tax)

A bright, clean, and modern office skyscraper interior. Sunlight streams through large windows, illuminating a spacious, minimalist office with glass walls and sleek, white furniture. Abstract, glowing white and blue lines, representing financial data flow, move through the air. The overall mood is professional, optimistic, and high-tech.

## Prompt 3 (Dashboard Tax - Cover)

A clean, abstract background for a financial report. A soft, white and light gray gradient with subtle, glowing blue lines forming a gentle, wave-like pattern. The design is minimalist, elegant, and professional, with a focus on clarity and readability.
